Privacy Demo Sans provenance (this header is not part of the OFL text below)

Privacy Demo Sans is a modified, renamed, and subsetted build derived from
the official Google Fonts Noto Sans KR variable TTF. The Reserved Font Name
is "Source"; this modified font does not use that name as its primary family.

Official source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanskr
